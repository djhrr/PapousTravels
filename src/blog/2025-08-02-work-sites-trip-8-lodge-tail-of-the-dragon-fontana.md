It was a Wednesday and it was time to plan the next ride. I decided to put some of this new AI to work to find a good ride and maybe even a place to stay the night. After a lot of prompts and multiple different apps, I found it. A reasonable priced lodge and campground that fit my needs. So I started planning. 2 days, 5 sites. Ambitious? Proven absolutely that! After some re-working we got it to 2 days, 3 sites in 7 hours on the 2nd day. Lots of riding and knocking out those sites would be great. Again, proven too ambitious. But this time it's ok! See because this time we had the dragon! Wow here we go again! Please calm down. 

The lodge is a campground that caters specifically to motorcyclists. It will cater to others but the main intent is, the riders of the dragon and the Cherohala Skyway. (As I was soon to find out!) The campground was purchased by the Smith family and a lot of updates are currently going on. The favorite update of mine are the single room cabins. The thing itself is about 10' x 10' and has just enough room for a bed, a fridge boot heaters and a shelving unit. It was way better than a tent. And a bonus is a covered awning to park the bike under out of the weather!

After getting checked in and a dinner at a local joint called Iron works grill. It was time to turn in for the night to get an early morning start. 
At 7:30AM the next morning I was out eating breakfast and on the way to, (what I thought was my first stop) Fontana Dam. But before that, the backstory. 
This was the first trip for many new experiments. For instance, I worked with a few AI apps to plan the trip and the thing that changed the whole trip mid run, I used a new app for navigation, (something I will sort of regret). Imagine riding a few roads, For giggles we will call them 441 and 72. For what I knew these roads helped take me to the famed Fontana dam. The largest concrete dam this side of the mighty Mississippi! The next part is hard to imagine if you never been there before but you dead end into 129...... Your GPS tells you to turn right..... And you do... 1 turn, 2 turns 14 turns and things start looking somewhat familiar.. I've been here.. Long ago but I've been here. A few more turns and it hits. 
Holy Hell I'm riding the dragon! (tail of the dragon for those that do not know) I rode this strip in the past. New bike, New me, and the lousy good for nothing person I rode it with last time is nowhere in sight!  It's time for new memories! And that is where my head went! New Memories!  And boy did I do just that!

I think the choice was clear at that point! The sites could wait for another trip. I knew 1 rout without the need for maps, and it was also an incredible ride in itself! Ladies and Gentlemen.. I present the Cherohala Skyway. 43-mile National Scenic Byway that connects Robbinsville, North Carolina, to Tellico Plains, Tennessee  It's known for its stunning mountain views, numerous overlooks, hiking trails, and historical significance. For me it will be remembered by not only the amazing ride and views but starting out I witnessed a sobering reality of watching an ambulance leave a scene of a biker accident without it's lights or siren.  If you know, you know. 😥

Not too quickly, but soon after, the thrills of riding the sweeping curves along the ridgelines. looking up and seeing riders, look in the mirror and seeing a line of riders. I was in a pack and I was in heaven.
