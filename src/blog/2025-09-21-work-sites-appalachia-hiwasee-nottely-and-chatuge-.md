A little bummed, but it was time to head to Nottely. Or so I thought. Not more than
three miles down the road I see a sign that says Dam that way. What Dam? I didn’t
see a dam on my plans in this area. But I am here and I know a sign when I see
one so turn I did! And rode. And rode. And rode and rode for almost 8 miles
before I came to what turned out to be Hiwasee dam.

lower the pool depending on the season. Visiting at the beginning of fall you
can already see the lowering that has occurred in preparation for the heavy
rain expected in the fall/early winter season.
