<p class="MsoNormal"><span style="mso-tab-count: 1;">                </span>Tellico
has a history, good or bad it has a story. Researching it I learned that
building it saved many lives through flood control and it has supplied much
needed spark to the area in home prices and opportunity. However there is a
larger than average amount of issue with what was buried under the water, and
how it happened. Largely political in nature, which I steer clear of here, so I
will simply say, if you are up for a ‘who is the good guy’ mystery? The history
of Tellico dam is a good study. <o:p></o:p>
