Wilbur and Watauga dams. I came for one and found the other.
Well sort of. It wasn’t until I was there that I realized that Watauga was down
the road. But let’s not get ahead of ourselves. Wilbur is the oldest TVA dam.
Originally built in 1917, it was rebuilt in 1952. It’s an impressive dam. It
was releasing water while I was there.<iframe allowfullscreen="" class="BLOG_video_class" height="266" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/dDqxRO-KSpA" width="320" youtube-src-id="dDqxRO-KSpA"></iframe><iframe allowfullscreen="" class="BLOG_video_class" height="266" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/aqbDutfl7fk" width="320" youtube-src-id="aqbDutfl7fk"></iframe>

Watauga has been closed to the public. You can get to the overlook and see the

that. But you get what you get and you don’t pitch a fit.
