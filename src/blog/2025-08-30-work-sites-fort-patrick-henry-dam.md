<p class="MsoNormal">Fort Patrick Henry Dam is one of the smallest dams we have. Ocoee
2 is the smallest. While it’s not a huge tourist attraction, it is a wonderfully peaceful place to visit. I found motorcyclists appreciate the dam if just for the location right outside the town. The scenery is just amazing.

<p class="MsoNormal">Much like Boone which is less than fifteen minutes away, FPHD was built in the 50’s, producing sixty
megawatts it will power the town of Kingsport with a little to spare. It’s the third
of the series of dams that include South Holston and Cherokee.
