Moto Camping
<a href="/assets/blog-images/Motocampprep__19_.JPEG" style="display: block; padding: 1em 0; text-align: center; "><img alt="" border="0" width="400" data-original-height="1536" data-original-width="2048" src="/assets/blog-images/Motocampprep__19_.JPEG"/></a>In an effort to find things to do with my time now that my precious boys are both now out of the house I thought about what I like to do. Well we know riding, but what else? That’s hard. I have not had even a day when I was not responsible for a minor since 1994 what do I like to do? Last thing I remember doing that I liked was camping in religious version of boy scouts, I think they called it royal rangers or something like that. Anyway I wonder if anyone has put the two together. Motorcycling and camping. Off to YouTube and hours later watching my 150th video of people that like the same things I like. 

If I know anything about rangers (scouts) it’s always be prepared. 

I also know, not to spend too much money on something until you are fully committed. 

So I set about preparing to try my hand at Moto Camping! <p/>
I pulled my tents from storage, then proceeded to tear the house apart looking for all my old camping stuff! I found I had a LOT of camping equipment saved up from small trips with friends over the years. But almost all of it was sized for 4 wheel camping. Enter the purge! 

I spent days setting up a camp site to the level that I liked, with the least amount of items included. 

Dropped the ‘favorite’ tent for the smaller, more practical tent. Found some cheap items that rounded out what I had and finally got it all done. The site is ready. I even made a test meal!<p/>

Time to pack! And first lesson! You have to pack incredibly tight. I had 4 bags and then smalls. Way too much for the bike. So everything came out and re-packed into 2 bags and then smalls. 
<a href="/assets/blog-images/Motocampprep__9_.JPEG" style="display: block; padding: 1em 0; text-align: center; "><img alt="" border="0" width="200" data-original-height="1536" data-original-width="2048" src="/assets/blog-images/Motocampprep__9_.JPEG"/></a><a href="/assets/blog-images/Motocampprep__7_.JPEG" style="display: block; padding: 1em 0; text-align: center; "><img alt="" border="0" height="200" data-original-height="2048" data-original-width="1536" src="/assets/blog-images/Motocampprep__7_.JPEG"/></a><a href="/assets/blog-images/Motocampprep__8_.JPEG" style="display: block; padding: 1em 0; text-align: center; "><img alt="" border="0" height="200" data-original-height="2048" data-original-width="1536" src="/assets/blog-images/Motocampprep__8_.JPEG"/></a>

Pulled down the saddlebags from storage for added room and packed the bike like I have never packed before! <p/>
<a href="/assets/blog-images/Motocampprep__15_.JPEG" style="display: block; padding: 1em 0; text-align: center; "><img alt="" border="0" width="320" data-original-height="1536" data-original-width="2048" src="/assets/blog-images/Motocampprep__15_.JPEG"/></a><a href="/assets/blog-images/Motocampprep__12_.JPEG" style="display: block; padding: 1em 0; text-align: center; "><img alt="" border="0" width="320" data-original-height="1536" data-original-width="2048" src="/assets/blog-images/Motocampprep__12_.JPEG"/></a>
Time for the first test ride!!! <p/>

Down the road about 5 miles and back, call it a shake out. I found some things I wanted to change. For instance I’ll increase the shocks on the bike, move some weight from the luggage rack to the back seat. Along with some things I forgot to add like a Pillow and off spray. Things you don’t think about until you are going down the road ransacking your mind! <p/>

Tomorrow I will be riding around the block then setting up the entire site at home as a test and then a few hours later field tear down, bike pack, small ride then (If no more changes) put things away till next week. <p/>

Next weekend I’m hoping to get my first test camping in for a night. <p/>
