Watts Bar Dam (<i>Hey watch your mouth!</i>)<P/>
Down the road from Watts Bar Nuclear is Watts Bar Dam. It’s an impressive dam, it does its job. I am struck by how clean it is. And the amount of energy created in these two sites side by side is quite amazing. <P/>
I didn’t spend long here. As I didn’t bring my fishing poles. (<i>Someone told me to… My bad!</i>) 
Oh one other thing that struck me upon leaving was with the dam is a park. And even though these sites are in the middle of nowhere, there is quite a bit of people around!
