It's basically a battery. The water is the current stored. Most nights, when the cost of power is low, (When more power is being put into the system than being used because most people are sleeping with their lights off) TVA pumps water from the river up into the bowl at the top of the mountain. 

<a href="/assets/blog-images/IMG_3639.JPG" imageanchor="1" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img border="0" data-original-height="4032" data-original-width="3024" height="640" src="/assets/blog-images/IMG_3639.JPG" width="480" /></a>

<span style="text-align: left;">During the day, when power demand is high they release water through turbines just like at a dam, and that creates additional power. But it doesn't end there, because that same water makes it's way through the rest of the river system creating power at each dam along the way. </span><span style="text-align: left;">
