Paradise is the only thing that has stayed the same with this site. It used to be a fossil plant, you know, burning that nasty coal! Gross! Give me a break! Anyway, during the last few years it has replaced all three fossil burners with natural gas turbines. I personally think why not use both? There are documented ways of cleaning coal clean and I love using natural gas, so...Why not? But well people that make more money than I do decided why not.
