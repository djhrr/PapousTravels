Southaven represents another facility that our organization didn't construct from the ground up. The plant was originally built by Cogentrix Energy. Our crews actually helped operate and run the facility for a period under contract before our enterprise officially purchased it outright in 2013.Southaven is a combined-cycle facility fueled by methane (natural gas). And as anyone working around heavy gas infrastructure can tell you, natural gas operations definitely have a distinct, pungent aroma—it stinks! But behind that distinct smell lies some of the most impressive thermal engineering on the entire grid.<a href="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhKILFKsNZ6AONuanq8n87_Afh7S5TBf7Of5CQWQXIr2_rEoyCN0yqVwxFCT3PDXW06jElJBMAQfyVR-cU3H4vyRwID4hj_DB_F0eYBdptE2DHy0p9bWDZ5F6CGwPIwRJYKYSm13XsRCT8HXnK6_PvPmWm3X8hIZyXIve4paP6rMjlKRXqnOz4igF3dqEA/s4032/IMG_3825.JPG" imageanchor="1" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img border="0" data-original-height="3024" data-original-width="4032" height="480" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhKILFKsNZ6AONuanq8n87_Afh7S5TBf7Of5CQWQXIr2_rEoyCN0yqVwxFCT3PDXW06jElJBMAQfyVR-cU3H4vyRwID4hj_DB_F0eYBdptE2DHy0p9bWDZ5F6CGwPIwRJYKYSm13XsRCT8HXnK6_PvPmWm3X8hIZyXIve4paP6rMjlKRXqnOz4igF3dqEA/w640-h480/IMG_3825.JPG" width="640" /></a>
The Southaven facility features three distinct power blocks engineered to extract every possible bit of energy out of the fuel it burns:Step 1: Natural gas is burned in primary gas turbines to generate initial high-voltage electricity.Step 2: Instead of venting the scorching hot exhaust gas into the atmosphere, the intense leftover thermal energy is captured by massive Heat Recovery Steam Generators (HRSGs).Step 3: The captured heat creates high-pressure steam, which is then routed into a secondary set of steam turbines to generate additional power.Efficiency Boost: By capturing and reusing the waste heat, combined-cycle plants generate roughly 50% more electricity from the exact same amount of fuel compared to standard simple-cycle units.<a href="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhsEYbS6GBMl2mvlM5goMWKcJn1PrFgp4PdtR2mTjWsGgCS7DkI6WHxSSlNR4k9-vmKMxqLLp5tBSqRq_LZnDdk8jCpTv2Zxd23Yr2sT-jR7bNda87lSpB6gGHFwEj-liEZjPnGgrBCOR01fOe_Q7aeg0nfB9HNEWuaV1JaxdMzIxUIAde0Z7z8LjU_ulI/s4032/IMG_3828.JPG" imageanchor="1" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img border="0" data-original-height="3024" data-original-width="4032" height="480" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhsEYbS6GBMl2mvlM5goMWKcJn1PrFgp4PdtR2mTjWsGgCS7DkI6WHxSSlNR4k9-vmKMxqLLp5tBSqRq_LZnDdk8jCpTv2Zxd23Yr2sT-jR7bNda87lSpB6gGHFwEj-liEZjPnGgrBCOR01fOe_Q7aeg0nfB9HNEWuaV1JaxdMzIxUIAde0Z7z8LjU_ulI/w640-h480/IMG_3828.JPG" width="640" /></a>
