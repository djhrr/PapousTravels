Clinch River | Oak Ridge

This is a case of killing 4 birds with 1 stone. I needed to test the bike after a recent fix, I wanted to check out the future Clinch River site, I wanted to see what I could see in Oak Ridge, given it's history and how it interlocks with ours. And finally, I wanted to do the Devils Triangle. Surprisingly, all 4 objectives achieved.
