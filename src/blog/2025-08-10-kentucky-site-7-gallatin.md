<p style="text-align: center;"> Gallatin Combustion / Tennessee / Fossil

<a href="/assets/blog-images/KY_8_Gal_Ten__6_.JPEG" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img border="0" data-original-height="1536" data-original-width="2048" height="300" src="/assets/blog-images/KY_8_Gal_Ten__6_.JPEG" width="400" /></a><p style="text-align: center;">I will be honest. By the time I got to Gallatin my mind was thinking about home. While still a 90 minute ride I was already there. To my defense I had been riding hours in 90°, I was beat. But I had a job to do, so on I trudged, through plant and even stopped in a secluded 'park' with a very old cemetery. 

<a href="/assets/blog-images/KY_8_Gal_Ten__7_.JPEG" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img border="0" data-original-height="1536" data-original-width="2048" height="300" src="/assets/blog-images/KY_8_Gal_Ten__7_.JPEG" width="400" /></a>This is the first site I visited that has both Fossil and Combustion on the same land. 
I like the concept! 
<a href="/assets/blog-images/KY_8_Gal_Ten__20_.JPEG" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img border="0" data-original-height="1536" data-original-width="2048" height="300" src="/assets/blog-images/KY_8_Gal_Ten__20_.JPEG" width="400" /></a>
The original turbines were the first of their kind when put in, while the later 4 are now considered the best of the best in the world. 
Pretty neat concept, sustained power with the fossil and immediate power with the combustion side. 
 <a href="/assets/blog-images/KY_8_Gal_Ten__21_.JPEG" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img border="0" data-original-height="1536" data-original-width="2048" height="300" src="/assets/blog-images/KY_8_Gal_Ten__21_.JPEG" width="400" /></a>OK so I'm hiding from the sun, You would too if you were fully cooked!
