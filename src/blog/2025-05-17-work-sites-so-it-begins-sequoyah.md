  The first work site destination is also the closest. Sequoyah. I work on so
  many computers in this site it's unreal, I also see this site as I go down the
  mountain into Soddy weekly, yet until May 17 2025, I had not visited. So I
  said what the hell and went!

  The first thing I noticed after being a little intimidated to approach even
  though I had credentials, was the noise generating electricity makes. It's a
  consistent hum around a 50-60 MHz. It’s all around you. I’m sure inside might
  be quieter.

<p />

  Anyway, as I rode around I saw a deer eating on grounds, (This will become a
  common thing at these sites as you will see.) And what I believe is a
  fantastic place to fish out the back, if I can do it without getting into too
  much trouble! 

[More about Sequoyah HERE!](https://www.tva.com/energy/our-power-system/nuclear/sequoyah-nuclear-plant)<P/>
<p />

So this was the first. I plan to visit as many as I can this summer.
